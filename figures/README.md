# Figures

- `model_comparison_7x8.png`: seven representative cases. Columns are GT, R(2+1)D-18, SlowFast-R50, R3D-18, Slow-R50, X3D, single-view X3D+MedSAM2, and five-view X3D+MedSAM2.
- `pipeline/`: one 30-frame video's intermediate inputs and outputs across all 14 stages of the adopted pipeline.

Green boxes are ground truth, yellow boxes are predictions, and the cyan border identifies the adopted five-view method.

The comparison cases were selected to visualize failure modes and the benefit of five-view fusion. They must not be interpreted as a random or exhaustive sample of the test set.
